```text

Fundador de Trail Running Cal
```

## Editing rules

- Preserve the founder introduction, product explanation, concrete brand-impact example, approved metrics, collaborative measurement, future availability, and single CTA.
- Tailor the sentence after `He visto que` to a real, timely company signal.
- Preserve the approved description of promotional placements unless a minimal, evidence-backed adaptation makes it more relevant to the brand.
- Mention only promotional formats that Trail Running Cal actually offers; do not invent placements.
- Do not mention partner names.
- Do not offer a 30-day pilot.
- Do not claim inventory is currently available.
- Avoid promotional subject-line language.
- Prefer removing a weak personalization to inventing or exaggerating one.
