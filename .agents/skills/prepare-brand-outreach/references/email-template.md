# Approved outreach email

## Subject

`[Marca] × Trail Running Cal`

## Body

```text
Hola [Nombre]!

Soy Ruben, fundador de [Trailrunningcal](https://www.trailrunningcal.com/es). Me he encontrado con [Marca] echando un ojo a [iniciativa o conjunto de señales concretas que demuestran una prioridad importante para la marca].

Estamos buscando marcas que están invirtiendo en reforzar su reconocimiento de marca en trail running y a las que podamos ayudarles a tener más presencia en la comunidad española. Estamos desarrollando un calendario de carreras de trail running consultado por ≈12.000 corredores mensuales (solo Cataluña), con la previsión de conseguir 60-100k a final de año en toda España.

Ya trabajamos con 2 marcas españolas, y probablemente abriremos nuevos espacios o acciones comerciales para más marcas en los próximos meses. ¿Tendría sentido valoraros cuando haya disponibilidad?

Si hay interés, os puedo enviar Media Kit con más métricas o tener una call con vosotros para explicaros con más detalle.

Gracias!
```

## Editing rules

- Preserve this progression: important company objective → Trail Running Cal as an easy solution → relevant scale and growth metrics → Spanish-brand social proof → clear CTA → offer of a Media Kit or call.
- Open naturally by explaining how Ruben found the company. Mention one or more specific initiatives that reveal an important objective the company is actively pursuing, such as Spanish expansion, race or event partnerships, a relevant product launch, distribution growth, or a category push.
- Prefer evidence showing an ongoing strategic focus over an isolated announcement. The recipient should immediately recognize their current work in the opening.
- Present Trail Running Cal as an uncomplicated way to gain recognition and presence in the Spanish trail-running community. Do not over-explain formats or campaign mechanics in the first email.
- Preserve these approved metrics and proof points: approximately 12,000 monthly runners in Catalonia, a forecast of 60–100k across Spain by year-end, and two existing Spanish brand relationships.
- Preserve the soft availability framing. Do not claim that inventory or commercial actions are currently available.
- Close by asking whether it would make sense to consider the company when availability opens, then offer either a Media Kit with more metrics or a call.
- Keep the email concise, clear, conversational, and founder-led. Prefer simple language such as `echando un ojo`, `valoraros`, and `tener una call` over corporate sales language.
- Do not use awards, funding, executive appointments, generic campaigns, vague praise, or isolated news as personalization unless they clearly reveal a commercially relevant priority that Trail Running Cal can support.
- Keep every factual claim in the personalization traceable to a recorded source.
- Do not mention partner names.
- Do not offer a 30-day pilot.
- Avoid promotional subject-line language.
- Prefer removing a weak personalization to inventing or exaggerating one.
