# Approved outreach email

## Subject

`[Marca] × Trail Running Cal`

## Body

```text
Hola [Nombre]!

Soy Ruben, fundador de [Trailrunningcal](https://www.trailrunningcal.com/es). Os escribo porque creo que podemos ayudaros a [objetivo estratégico concreto], especialmente ahora que [señal verificable que lo sustenta].

Trail Running Cal es un calendario de carreras de trail running que actualmente llega a [métrica vigente y ámbito geográfico obtenidos de product-context], con una audiencia que está buscando y planificando activamente sus próximas carreras.

Ya trabajamos con 2 marcas españolas, y probablemente abriremos nuevos espacios o acciones comerciales para más marcas en los próximos meses. ¿Tendría sentido valoraros cuando haya disponibilidad?

Si hay interés, os puedo enviar Media Kit con más métricas o tener una call con vosotros para explicaros con más detalle.

Gracias!
```

## Editing rules

- Preserve this progression: Ruben's introduction + one objective-first strategic sentence → Trail Running Cal as an easy solution → current verified scale → Spanish-brand social proof → clear CTA → offer of a Media Kit or call.
- Make the first substantive sentence answer `¿por qué os escribo y qué os puedo ayudar a conseguir?`. State exactly one strategic objective using `creo que podemos ayudaros a...`; subordinate the supporting signal with wording such as `especialmente ahora que...`.
- Keep the dynamic opening to one sentence whenever practical and no more than two. Do not lead with `me he encontrado con`, company praise, a news item, or a list of initiatives.
- Choose only one supported objective: enter or grow in Spain; build awareness for a new product, range, or category; strengthen trail-running positioning; extend a sponsorship or activation; or support a new Spanish distribution or sales channel.
- Prefer evidence showing an ongoing strategic focus over an isolated announcement. The recipient should immediately recognize both the initiative and its likely commercial relevance.
- Treat the objective as a hypothesis unless the company has stated it explicitly. Do not present an inferred internal goal as fact.
- Present Trail Running Cal as an uncomplicated way to gain recognition and presence in the Spanish trail-running community. Do not over-explain formats or campaign mechanics in the first email.
- Read audience size, geographic scope, and expansion status from the installed `product-context` skill when composing each draft. It is the canonical source; never combine its values with older drafts or figures from another skill. Omit forecasts unless Ruben has supplied or confirmed a current one.
- Preserve the approved proof point that Trail Running Cal already works with two Spanish brands, unless newer user-provided context supersedes it. Do not name them.
- Preserve the soft availability framing. Do not claim that inventory or commercial actions are currently available.
- Close by asking whether it would make sense to consider the company when availability opens, then offer either a Media Kit with more metrics or a call.
- Keep the email concise, clear, conversational, and founder-led. Prefer simple language such as `valoraros` and `tener una call` over corporate sales language.
- Promise only outcomes Trail Running Cal can credibly influence: awareness, discovery, consideration, adoption, or entry into Spain. Mention sales only when proposing a concrete measurable conversion or affiliate format; never promise increased sales.
- If the evidence concerns expansion into Europe, France, or Southern Europe, connect the proposal only to the Spanish part of that strategy. Never imply that Trail Running Cal provides reach outside Spain.
- Do not use awards, funding, executive appointments, generic campaigns, vague praise, or isolated news as personalization unless they clearly reveal a commercially relevant priority that Trail Running Cal can support.
- Keep every factual claim in the personalization traceable to a recorded source.
- Do not offer a 30-day pilot.
- Avoid promotional subject-line language.
- Prefer removing a weak personalization to inventing or exaggerating one.

## Opening examples

```text
Os escribo porque creo que podemos ayudaros a dar visibilidad en España a vuestra nueva línea de textil, especialmente ahora que estáis ampliando vuestra oferta más allá de las gorras.

Os escribo porque creo que podemos ayudaros a ganar reconocimiento entre corredores españoles durante vuestra entrada en el mercado, especialmente ahora que habéis incorporado un distribuidor para España.

Os escribo porque creo que podemos ayudaros a reforzar el posicionamiento de [Marca] en trail running, especialmente ahora que estáis invirtiendo en [patrocinio o activación concreta].
```

Use examples as structural guidance only. Never reuse their facts without company-specific evidence.
