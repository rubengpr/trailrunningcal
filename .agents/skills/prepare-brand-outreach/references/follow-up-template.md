# Approved follow-up outreach

Use this reference only after the skill establishes `follow-up` mode from the complete Gmail thread and Notion history.

## Eligibility and cadence

Classify the opportunity before drafting:

- `draft now`: Ruben sent the latest meaningful message, no later human reply exists through any known channel, and the cadence permits another touch.
- `wait until DATE`: the previous message is too recent or an out-of-office response supplies a return date. Wait until two working days after the return date.
- `Ruben owes reply`: the latest meaningful message came from the lead.
- `close or park`: the lead received the closing follow-up, opted out, declined, or has received three unanswered follow-ups without a new strategic reason to continue.
- `research needed`: the thread, recipient, channel history, or strategic point cannot be established confidently.

Default cadence when the user has not supplied another policy:

1. First follow-up: 5–7 working days after the initial message.
2. Second follow-up: 7–10 working days after the first follow-up; add new value or a more concrete proposal.
3. Closing follow-up: 10–14 working days after the second follow-up; request a simple disposition and then stop.

Never treat an automated acknowledgement, delivery notification, newsletter, or out-of-office response as a human reply. Never use automation as permission to ignore its stated return date.

## Strategic-point gate

Select exactly one verifiable signal from:

- Spanish or European expansion;
- a relevant product launch or category push;
- trail-running positioning or investment;
- an athlete, race, team, or circuit sponsorship;
- distributor, store, or channel entry;
- a relevant campaign or activation.

Build the point as:

```text
Verifiable fact → strategic objective → how Trail Running Cal helps
```

Before drafting, establish internally:

```text
Category:
Fact:
Source and date:
Strategic objective:
Trail Running Cal contribution:
Final sentence:
Confidence: high | medium | low
```

Draft only at high or medium confidence. The final sentence must state an outcome for the company, not merely mention an event. If the sentence could be sent unchanged to a direct competitor, it is too generic. If no valid point exists, return `RESEARCH NEEDED` rather than inventing one.

Examples of valid outcomes:

- reinforce a relevant product launch among trail runners;
- gain awareness during expansion into Spain;
- consolidate the brand's trail-running positioning;
- extend the value of an athlete, race, team, or circuit sponsorship;
- support entry through a new Spanish distributor or sales channel.

## First follow-up

### Spanish template

```text
Hola {nombre},

Estamos comenzando a asignar las 2–3 colaboraciones de marca disponibles para Q4 de 2026 y durante 2027, coincidiendo con el próximo cierre de presupuestos.

Creo que Trailrunningcal os puede ayudar a {objetivo estratégico concreto}.

¿Te envío el media kit con formatos, cifras y varias propuestas, o debería hablar con otra persona del equipo?

Mil gracias,
Ruben
```

Translate naturally when the established working language is Catalan, English, or another language. Preserve the meaning and firmness rather than translating mechanically.

### Editing rules

- Target 50–75 words and a reading time of roughly 7–12 seconds.
- Reply in the existing Gmail thread.
- Do not reintroduce Ruben or explain Trail Running Cal again.
- Do not repeat traffic metrics from the initial message.
- Do not resend or attach the Media Kit; ask permission to send it.
- Include exactly one validated strategic outcome.
- Use one low-friction CTA: send the Media Kit or redirect Ruben to the responsible person.
- For a generic inbox, ask only for the person responsible for collaborations, sponsorships, or media in Spain.
- Preserve the limited-capacity statement only while the 2–3-partner policy, Q4 2026/2027 planning horizon, and budget-cycle framing remain true.
- Never claim that spaces are nearly full, already reserved, or guaranteed to sell out without evidence.
- Avoid `just checking in`, generic praise, unsupported promises, artificial urgency, and claims such as `multiply your visibility`.

## Later follow-ups

Do not repeat the first follow-up verbatim.

- Second follow-up: introduce one new decision-relevant element, such as a concrete activation concept, relevant placement, measurement approach, or planning question. Keep the ask narrow.
- Closing follow-up: ask whether the opportunity is not relevant, should be revisited in a named period, or belongs with another person. State politely that the conversation will be closed for now if there is no response.

Never send more than three unanswered follow-ups without explicit user approval and a new evidence-backed reason.

## Draft-only rule

Create a reviewable Gmail reply draft. Never send, schedule, or enroll it in an automated sequence. Creating a draft must not change the Notion commercial state or contact dates.
